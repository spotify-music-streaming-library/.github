## Music Streaming Overview

Spotify organises content into playlists, albums, and saved shows. The recommendation engine suggests based on listening history, and discovery surfaces appear on the home screen. Playback continues across devices through the connect feature.

Users build playlists for focus blocks, save podcasts for commutes, and share collaborative lists for events. Spotify features that come up include collaborative playlists, the equaliser on mobile, and offline mode. A Spotify download is usually about having the app on a device where listening happens.
